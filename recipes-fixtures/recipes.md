## Recipe 1
Closer to the Heart Red IPA All Grain Recipe
(expected figures)

OG: 1.061
FG: 1.016
ABV: 6.0%
IBU: 60
Volume: 20 litres

Ingredients

4kg Pale malt
1kg Crystal (60L) malt
100g Roasted Barley*
60g Centennial pellets
55g Amarillo pellets
80g Cascade pellets
2 packs US-05 Ale yeast

Method

Heat 25.6L water to 71°C. You are shooting for a mash temperature of 67°C.
Immerse the grain bag in the water and stir the grist to ensure it is fully wetted.
Mash with occasional stirring for 1 hour.
Lift the grain bag out of the kettle and allow to drain. You should have 23L of 1.054 wort.
Once boiling, add 30g Centennial hops for a 60 minute boil.
With 30 minutes left on the boil, add 25g Amarillo hops.
With 15 minutes left on the boil, add 50g Cascade hops.
At end of boil check volume and gravity, you may need to add water to hit 20L.
Chill wort to 20°C and pitch yeast.
Allow 2 weeks for fermentation and maturation until dry hopping in fermenter with 30g Centennial, 30g Amarillo, 30g Cascade hops in a hop bag.
After 1 week, remove hop bag and bottle or keg as usual.
*Note: Substituting Simpsons Red Rye (100L) for the Caramel 60L makes a great Red Rye IPA.

## Recipe 6
Re: Extract oatmeal stout - what I did last weekend

Posted by hoppy_dave, Sat 14 Mar

Long time lurker. A few people asked for the recipe after the swap meet so here it is, 5 gal batch, stovetop partial boil (3 gal) topped up in the fermenter.

Steeped 1 lb of flaked oats with 8 oz roasted barley, 8 oz chocolate malt and 12 oz crystal 60 in about 2.5 gal at 155F for 30 mins. Oats don't really convert on their own without base malt, I know, but it's mostly for body. Pulled the bag, brought it to a boil, killed the heat and stirred in 6 lb of Muntons dark liquid malt extract plus 1 lb of light DME. Back to the boil.

Hops were dead simple: 1.5 oz East Kent Goldings at 60 and 0.5 oz Fuggles at 15. Threw in half a tsp of Irish moss at 15 as well. Also added 8 oz lactose at flameout because the missus likes it sweet, you could leave it out.

Topped up with cold water to 5 gal and pitched a vial of White Labs WLP002 English Ale at 66F. Sluggish for the first 12 hours, then took off.

OG 1.058, FG 1.020. Next time I'd drop the chocolate malt to 6 oz, it's a touch acrid.

## Recipe 8
==============================================
 KIT SHEET  |  WEST COAST IPA  |  23 L
 OG 1.064   FG 1.011   IBU 65   EBC 12   ~7.0%
==============================================

WHAT'S IN THE BOX
[ ] Bag A       Pale ale malt, crushed ............ 5.40 kg
[ ] Bag B       Munich malt, crushed .............. 0.50 kg
                Carapils, crushed ................. 0.25 kg
[ ] Bag C       Dextrose (brewing sugar) .......... 0.30 kg
[ ] Hop pack 1  Magnum ............................ 20 g     (60 min)
[ ] Hop pack 2  Simcoe ............................ 30 g     (10 min)
[ ] Hop pack 3  Mosaic + Citra .................... 40 g + 40 g   (whirlpool 20 min @ 80 °C)
[ ] Hop pack 4  Citra ............................. 50 g     (dry hop, day 3)
[ ] Hop pack 5  Mosaic + Simcoe ................... 40 g + 30 g   (dry hop, day 7)
[ ] Yeast       LalBrew Verdant IPA ............... 1 x 11 g sachet
[ ] Finings     Protafloc tablet .................. 1

NOT INCLUDED: water, sanitiser, CO2, priming sugar.

BREW DAY
1. Mash bags A + B at 65 °C for 60 min, mash out at 76 °C.
2. Boil 60 min. Hop pack 1 at start of boil. Bag C and the Protafloc tablet at 15 min. Hop pack 2 at 10 min.
3. Flame out, cool to 80 °C, add hop pack 3 and hold 20 min.
4. Chill to 18 °C, pitch the yeast dry onto the wort.
5. Hop pack 4 on day 3 of fermentation, hop pack 5 on day 7. Cold crash on day 10, package on day 12.

## Recipe 9
Belgian Witbier - all grain, 20 L into the fermenter
Mash 50 C / 10 min, 67 C / 60 min, 76 C mash out. 60 min boil. Target OG 1.048, FG 1.010, 15 IBU.

Grain
2.2 kg Pilsner malt
1.8 kg Flaked wheat
0.3 kg Flaked oats

Hops
20 g Hallertau Mittelfrüh (3.8% AA) - 60 min
10 g Saaz (3.5% AA) - 5 min

Spices (all in the last 5 min of the boil unless noted)
15 g coriander seed, freshly crushed
12 g dried bitter orange peel (Curacao)
10 g fresh sweet orange zest - at flameout

Water treatment (soft water, into the mash)
3 g calcium chloride
2 g gypsum
4 ml lactic acid 80%, aiming for mash pH 5.3

Yeast
White Labs WLP400 Belgian Wit Ale, 1 vial (make a 1 L starter the day before)

Ferment at 20 C, let it free-rise to 23 C after day 3. Keep it cloudy, no finings.
